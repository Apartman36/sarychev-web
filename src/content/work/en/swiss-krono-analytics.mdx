## Context

`swiss-krono-analytics-mvp` is a local analytics MVP for cleaning, monitoring, and forecasting sales data.

## Problem

Business data workflows need a clear path from raw data to monitoring and forecasting outputs.

## Approach

The project frames analytics as a compact MVP: clean the data, monitor the important signals, forecast where useful, and present reports.

## Result

The case demonstrates focused analytics MVP delivery for a domain workflow.
