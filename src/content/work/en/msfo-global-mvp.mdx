## Context

`msfo-global-mvp` is a business MVP for IFRS/MSFO-style financial workflows. It is included because AI integration work still depends on practical product delivery.

## Problem

Financial workflows often need clean import paths, understandable reports, charts, and export-ready outputs. A useful MVP has to respect that operational shape.

## Constraints

- Keep the interface focused on business workflow, not decoration.
- Make imported data visible and reviewable.
- Support reporting and chart-based interpretation.

## Approach

The project frames financial data as an internal tool workflow: import, inspect, report, visualize, and prepare outputs.

## Implementation highlights

- Data import and reporting-oriented UI.
- Charts for quick interpretation.
- MVP scope focused on usable workflow rather than broad platform features.

## Result

The project shows Antonii's ability to turn a domain workflow into a usable internal tool.

## What it demonstrates

Business MVP delivery, frontend product thinking, data workflow design, and reporting interfaces.
